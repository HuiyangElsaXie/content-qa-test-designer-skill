## Rubric and decision — defined before fixtures
Hard checks are separate from style scores:
- F1: Each material claim must be traceable to supplied evidence. Missing evidence makes the affected case Needs review; established contradictions make it Reject. Unsupported claims cannot pass on style merit.
- F2: Price and materials remain unresolved. Preserve both S1 and S2. Neither version earns Accept or Reject solely for taking one side; require owner resolution.
- F3: Offer timing and availability words, including weekend, soon, now and still available, need applicable dated evidence. Missing support is Needs review.
- R1: The line must serve the supplied sketching invitation task. A clearly unrelated line fails.
- R2: Preserve mandatory fixed text only where explicitly required. No fixed wording is required here. Keyword matches neither prove truth nor invalidate paraphrases.

For each subjective dimension, record a 0–2 score, textual evidence and uncertainty during a future review:

| Dimension | 0: violates | 1: partial | 2: meets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Required information | Misstates the class or omits an explicitly required element | Sketching purpose unclear | Clearly communicates the class/invitation purpose at line level |
| Task usefulness | Unrelated or unusable | Relevant but unclear | Useful invitation line |
| Channel fit | Unsuitable for a public invitation | Needs adaptation to the eventual channel | Readable public-facing line; exact-channel uncertainty recorded |
| Style | Confusing or misleading | Understandable but awkward | Clear, natural and concise |

Accept: all hard checks pass and every subjective dimension meets 2. Reject: a demonstrated factual contradiction or clear task failure, or a subjective 0 with evidence. Needs review: missing/contradictory source prevents adjudication, a subjective 1, or uncertainty prevents a confident decision. Factual uncertainty cannot be overridden by good prose. Labels below are design intent, not observed grades or scores.
